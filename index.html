Get MixR

MixR is a community app for university students. You must be 18 or older.

MixR is in public beta. Pick your phone below.

iPhone: join the beta on TestFlight Android: download the beta (APK)

iPhone: install Apple's free TestFlight app when asked, then tap Accept and Install.

Android: open the downloaded file and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser if Android asks. Google Play Protect may show a warning because the beta is not from the Play Store; choose "Install anyway". Some purchases may not work in the beta.
